Judge the final answer against these observable criteria.

1. The plan inventories our own product from the code, tests or what actually runs, and treats our docs, README or roadmap as claims to check, not as the inventory.
2. The plan looks beyond direct competitors to what users choose instead (spreadsheets, in-house scripts, consultancies, neighbouring tools).
3. The answer says every finding about a competitor is marked by how it was known (page fetched with its date, search snippet only, from memory, looked for and not found).
4. Prices and feature claims are taken from the vendor's own pages, and unverified claims that would drive the decision are listed with how to settle each (trial, screenshot, sales question).
5. The hand-back ends with what to copy, what to do better and what not to copy, and its size is matched to a single-feature question, not a full market report.

PASS if at least 4 of the 5 criteria hold. FAIL otherwise. Name the criteria that failed.
